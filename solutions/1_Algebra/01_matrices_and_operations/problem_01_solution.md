

Given matrices:
$$

A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & -1 & 3 \\ 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad B = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ -2 & 3 \\ 4 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

---

### **1. State the sizes of matrices $A$ and $B$**

* Matrix size is written in the form $\text{rows} \times \text{columns}$.
* **Matrix $A$:** Has 2 rows and 3 columns, so its size is **$2 \times 3$**.


* **Matrix $B$:** Has 3 rows and 2 columns, so its size is **$3 \times 2$**.



---

### **2. Read off the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$**

*(In index notation $m_{ij}$, $i$ represents the row and $j$ represents the column)*

* **$a_{12}$:** Entry at row 1, column 2 of matrix $A$: **$-1$**

* **$a_{23}$:** Entry at row 2, column 3 of matrix $A$: **$5$**

* **$b_{21}$:** Entry at row 2, column 1 of matrix $B$: **$-2$**

* **$b_{32}$:** Entry at row 3, column 2 of matrix $B$: **$1$**


---

### **3. Write the second row of $A$ and the first column of $B$ as vectors**

* **The second row of $A$ (as a row vector):**
$$

\mathbf{r}_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 4 & 5 \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{or} \quad [0, 4, 5]
$$

* **The first column of $B$ (as a column vector):**
$$

\mathbf{c}_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ -2 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}

$$
